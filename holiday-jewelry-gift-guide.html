Frequently asked questions

What is the safest jewelry gift if I don't know their style?

Earrings or a bracelet are usually the safest choice because they need little or no sizing. Simple gold hoops or a fine chain bracelet suit many styles.

How do I find a ring size without asking?

Borrow a ring they already wear on the same finger and compare it with a ring sizer or the retailer's size chart. If you are unsure, choose earrings, a necklace or a bracelet instead.

Are lab-grown diamonds real diamonds?

Yes. Lab-grown diamonds have the same chemical composition and crystal structure as mined diamonds. They are grown in a laboratory instead of being mined, and retailers usually describe them as lab-grown on the product page.

Can a jewelry gift be returned or exchanged?

It depends on the retailer. Read the return and exchange policy on the retailer's own site before you order, and ask whether the recipient can exchange the item without the original receipt.

How early should I order jewelry for the holidays?

Order early, because delivery times vary by retailer, destination and whether a piece is made to order. Check each retailer's own holiday shipping cut-off before you buy.

Do the prices on this page include Black Friday or Cyber Monday discounts?

No. The prices here are the listed prices we have on file as of November 2, 2026, and we do not list sale prices or promo codes. Any seasonal offer is set by the retailer, so check the retailer's own page for the price you will pay.
